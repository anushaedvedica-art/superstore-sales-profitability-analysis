# SuperStore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Excel to analyze e-commerce sales transactions and evaluate revenue, profitability, regional performance, and product performance.

The objective is to turn transaction-level data into meaningful business insights that help stakeholders understand sales performance and monitor key business metrics.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Compare revenue across geographic regions.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare revenue across product categories and products.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Excel formulas and functions
- PivotTables
- Charts and dashboard design
- Data cleaning and validation

## Dataset Overview

The cleaned dataset contains **499 transaction records** with fields including:

- Order ID and order date
- Customer ID
- Product and category
- Region
- Quantity and unit price
- Discount
- Revenue, cost, and profit

## Analysis Included

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trends
Examines revenue by month to identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 2. Regional Performance
Compares revenue across North, East, West, and South regions.

### 3. Category Performance
Evaluates revenue across product categories.

### 4. Product Performance
Compares revenue generated by individual products.

### 5. Executive Dashboard
Summarizes key business metrics and visualizes sales performance in one place.

## Data Quality Notes

- The cleaned dataset contains 499 transaction records.
- Two records have invalid or unavailable order dates. These records remain in the dataset but cannot be reliably assigned to a month.
- The monthly analysis currently includes 497 transactions with usable dates and reports revenue of $130,068.79.
- The dashboard reports revenue of $131,404.67 across all 499 records.
- Regional summary totals may differ from the dashboard total. These differences require reconciliation before the regional figures are used as final business results.
